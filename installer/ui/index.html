Services (claude.ai connectors)

Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Slack come from your claude.ai account (claude.ai → Settings → Connectors). Click [ authorize ] on a service: connectors open their approval page on claude.ai (counts from the next Claude session), other MCP servers run their OAuth in your browser. One at a time, only what you want.
